<h2><strong>I am selling my business- What are my Tax Liabilities and How do I maximize my Profits?</strong></h2>
<p>When you are selling your business as a sole trader or a partner you will have to pay Capital Gains Tax on any profits that you make.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>On the other hand, you won't be paying CGT. If you run your business as a limited company and you make a gain on its sale instead you pay <a href="https://www.taxaccolega.co.uk/small-businesses/sole-trader-or-limited-company">corporation tax</a>.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>In this blog we will discuss how you can make use of the reliefs available to you if you sell your sole trader or a partnership business to reduce your Capital Gains tax and therefore maximize your profits.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><strong>What is Capital Gains Tax</strong></p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><a href="https://www.taxaccolega.co.uk/resources/tax-rates-and-allowances/capital-gains-tax">Capital Gains Tax</a> is the tax on the profit when you sell something which has increased in value.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>You have to pay capital gains if you make a profit when you sell( or dispose of) all or part of the business.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><strong>Annual Exempt Amount</strong></p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>There is an annual exempt amount available which is £12 300 you won't have to pay any taxes if your gain is within the annual exempt amount.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>You will be able to utilize the annual exempt amount most efficiently if you sell or dispose of your business in parts. </p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><strong>What are the reliefs available to me if I am selling my business?</strong></p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>There are certain eligibility criteria for each relief and if you are eligible you can get the relief depending on your circumstances and you can save on your taxes by</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:list --></p>
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>      ● paying the reduced percentage of the taxes</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<p><!-- /wp:list --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>or</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:list --></p>
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>      ● by delaying certain taxes that you pay for example capital gains tax</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<p><!-- /wp:list --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p> Let's look at the following reliefs available to you and see if you are eligible and we accountants in Croydon Can help you claim them .</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><strong>Business Asset Disposal Relief:</strong></p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>You can pay the reduced amount of CGT(10 %)instead of the <a href="https://www.taxaccolega.co.uk/factsheets/capital-taxes/capital-gains-tax">normal rates </a>if the following applies to you</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:list --></p>
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>      ● You are a <a href="https://www.taxaccolega.co.uk/small-businesses/sole-trader-or-limited-company">sole trader </a>or a partner</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>      ● You have owned the business for at least 2 years.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<p><!-- /wp:list --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><strong>Gift Hold Over Relief:</strong></p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>If you are just giving away your asset you won't be paying any taxes on it. Instead CGT will be paid when the person you gave your asset sells it.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>You can claim this relief when you have used the business asset for business purposes as a sole trader or a partner.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><strong>Delaying the Payment of Capital Gains Tax:</strong></p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><strong>You can claim incorporation Relief:</strong></p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>You can delay the payment of CGT if you transfer all your business and its assets in return for the shares in a company.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>This is not actual selling, it is when you transfer your business to a company.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><strong>How do I claim</strong></p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>You do not have to claim incorporation relief you will get it automatically,</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><strong>Business Asset disposal Relief:</strong></p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>You can claim this relief if you dispose of your assets and replace them with the new ones within 3 years of disposing of them.</p>
