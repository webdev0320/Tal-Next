  How do I report my loans and <br>Grants given during Pandemic 


  in the self assessment tax return?
				
		<figure></figure>
<p><!-- /wp:image --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>Loans which were granted to the self-employed during the Pandemic to support their businesses are taxable and therefore they need to be reported in the self assessment tax return.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>There is an exception to this. If the payments made to an individual are by the council and they are the support payments meaning that they were meant to support the council tax payments or the housing benefit you won't have to report them in the self assessment tax return . This is because they are counted as the welfare payments and therefore they are not taxable.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>You will have to report if you received money from the following grant:</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:list --></p>
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>      ● Self employment income support scheme</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>      ● Test and trace or self isolation payments</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>      ● Coronavirus job retention scheme</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>      ● Eat out to help out</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>      ● Coronavirus statutory sick pay rebate</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>      ● Coronavirus Business Support Grants</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<p><!-- /wp:list --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><strong>Filling the self employment pages of your tax return</strong></p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>You will be filling self employment - short pages if your turnover including the taxable coronavirus support scheme payments was less than £ 85 000. If the turnover is more than this figure and your business is more complicated for example you have changed your accounting period or your basis period is different from your accounting date etc.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><strong>SEISS payments</strong></p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>You will include this in box 27.1</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>If the SEISS payment that you receive relates to self employment and partnership. Apportion the amount on a just and reasonable basis. The total of which should equal the amount received by the individual.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><strong>Other Coronavirus support payments</strong></p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>You will have to include the payments from other loans and grants in box 10.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>In box 10 you will only include the payments that you received and retained.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>If you received an incorrect payment which you were not entitled to and you have returned it you will not include it in box 10.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>If you received a payment from the grant which you were entitled to and you have not returned it either you should include the incorrectly claimed amount in SA 100.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>If you have received a grant under eat out to help out scheme and you are VAT registered don't add the VAT amount when including the amount in box 10.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><strong>Trading Allowance</strong></p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>An allowance of £1000 is available for every business. This means that if your earnings for the year are less than £1000 you don't have to report it to HMRC, pay any taxes and fill in the self assessment tax return. However, if your income from self employment and all the miscellaneous income includes SEISS grant even if it is less than £1000 you will have to report it in the self assessment - short pages.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><strong>Will I be including the Loans and Grants in Calculating my Trading Profits</strong></p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>You have 2 options to calculate the trading profits</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} --></p>
<ol><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>      1. </strong>Include the total income and deduct the trading allowance. In this case you cannot claim any other allowable expenses against your profit.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>     2. </strong>Deduct allowable expenses and allowances from the total income but you cannot claim the trading allowance in this case. The total receipts from self employment will include the figure from box 10( other Coronavirus Support Payments), however it won't include payments from box 27.1(SEISS grant)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
