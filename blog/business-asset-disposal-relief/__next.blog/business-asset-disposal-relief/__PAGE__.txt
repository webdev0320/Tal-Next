  Business Asset Disposal Relief

				
		<figure><img src="https://www.taxaccolega.co.uk/img/blog/business-asset-disposal.jpg" alt="" /></figure>
<p><!-- /wp:image --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>Business Asset disposal relief which was previously known as entrepreneur`s relief is the relief given to individuals who are working as <a href="https://www.taxaccolega.co.uk/factsheets/starting-up-in-business/business-structures-which-should-i-use"><strong>sole traders</strong></a> or partners and have owned the business for 2 years.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>If an individual who is fulfilling the above mentioned conditions, sells the business in the tax tear 2020/2021 and afterwards they will pay <a href="https://www.taxaccolega.co.uk/factsheets/capital-taxes/capital-gains-tax">capital gains tax</a> at a reduced rate of 10% on the first £ 1 million of the gains profit that they will make on selling the business.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>This life time allowance of entrepreneur's relief has been slashed in the 2020 budget which was previously £10million. So if you have already used your £ 1 million allowance you won't be able to claim relief.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><strong>Do buy to let landlords qualify for entrepreneur relief?</strong> The answer is maybe not. This is because HMRC clearly mentions that to qualify for the entrepreneur's relief the 'business' will include any trade or profession or vocation but it will not include the letting of the property unless it is holiday lettings unless it is furnished holiday lettings in UK or EEA.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>

<p><strong>How to calculate the relief?</strong></p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>Suppose all your Gains qualify for the relief. The gain will be the difference between what you sold the assets for and how much you paid to buy it. (In some cases we will use the market value as well)</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>Deduct the tax free allowance of £12300(for individuals and personal representatives) and £6150 for the trustees If you are a basic rate tax payer you will have to use your basic rat band first and pay 10 % on all the gains.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>Once calculated the gain we will look for the expenses which can be deducted such as SDLT or advertising expense.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>Other situations in which the CGT can be deffered is making use of rollover relief where the proceeds of a business is reinvested in new business assets or when the business assets are transferred to the spouse or a civil partner instead of being sold.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p><strong>How to claim the relief and what are the deadlines?</strong></p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>The relief can be claimed in the self-assessment tax return in the 'Capital Gains summary section'. The deadline is the 31 of January following the disposal. For example, if the disposal was made in Sep 2020 the last date to make the claim would be 31 Jan 2023</p>
