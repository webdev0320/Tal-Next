					<h2 id="ai-automation-accounting-schools" style="font-size:28px; font-weight:400; color:#1d3c45;">
    <strong>AI and automation in accounting schools
</strong>
</h2>
    <p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify; line-height:1.8;">
        AI stands for Artificial Intelligence. It is a term that describes machines or computer software that can “think” and “learn” like human beings.<strong> Role of AI in modern accounting education</strong> Here are some key roles of AI in accounting
    </p>
    <p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify; line-height:1.8; padding-left:20px;">
●   Automatically scan and process invoices<br>
●   Match and sort receipts and payments
<br>
●   Identify errors or fraud more effectively
<br>
●   Use data analysis to give business insights
<br>
●   Prepare tax returns and reports faster
</p>
<p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify; line-height:1.8;">
       This takes less time, reduces errors, and allows accountants to focus on more important tasks like advising clients and helping companies grow.
    </p>
    <h3 id="how-ai-changing-accounting" style="font-size:20px; font-weight:400; color:#1d3c45; padding-left:20px;">
    <strong>How AI is changing accounting
</strong>
</h3>
<p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify; line-height:1.8;padding-left:20px;">
      AI is now a big part of accounting, learning accounting has changed too. In the past, students essentially learned to post transactions, make up accounts, and comply with tax law. All those are still necessary but these days’ students also learn <strong>accounting curriculum and AI integration</strong>
    </p>
        <p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify; line-height:1.8; padding-left:20px;">
●   How to use cloud-based accounting programs like <strong>Xero and QuickBooks</strong>
<br>
●   How to analyse and verify reports produced by AI systems
<br>
●   How to utilise data for better business decisions
<br>
●   How to be responsible and ethical when using technology
</p>
        <p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify; line-height:1.8; padding-left:20px;">
       This helps new accountants keep up to date and ready for modern work environments.
    </p>
