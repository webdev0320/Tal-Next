  R&amp;D Tax Relief Regulations 2025


  Merged RDEC &amp; ERIS 
				
<p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify;">
You might be eligible for <strong>Research and Development (R&D) tax relief</strong> if your company makes investments in technology, innovation, or process enhancements. <strong>HMRC</strong> has made significant changes to the way <strong>R&D tax relief operates in the UK</strong> as of <strong> 1 April 2024.</strong> Two new schemes have taken the place of the previous RDEC and SME programmes:
</p>
<p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify; line-height:1.8; padding-left:20px;">
●   <strong>Integrated R&D Expenditure Credit (RDEC)</strong>
<br>
●   <strong>ERIS (Enhanced R&D Intensive Scheme) plan</strong>
</p>
<p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify;">
<strong>Taxaccolega</strong> discusses the implications of these changes for your company, who is eligible to make a claim, how much you stand to gain, and how to choose the best plan for you in this blog. What’s new in from <strong>April 2024?</strong>
</p>
<p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify;">
The government has combined and simplified the prior <strong>R&D schemes.</strong> Now, depending on your company size and <strong>R&D </strong>intensity, you may claim under.

<p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify;">
For the majority of companies, the merged RDEC scheme
<br>
The merged R&D Expenditure Credit <strong>(RDEC)</strong> scheme is now the default for most UK businesses. 20% of your R&D expenditures may be credited if you are engaged in qualifying R&D work. It is included in your income, so it is taxed. Depending on your corporation tax rate your net benefit after taxes ranges from <strong>14.7% to 16.2%.</strong>
</p>
<p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify;">
<strong>✅ Principal attributes:
</strong>
</p>
<p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify; line-height:1.8; padding-left:20px;">
●   Available to businesses of all sizes
<br>
●   Suitable for businesses that make money as well as those that don’t
<br>
●   Staff, supplies, and certain subcontractor expenses may be included
<br>
●   Subject to a PAYE/NIC cap
</p>
<p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify;">
<strong>For instance:
</strong>
</p>
<p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify;">
If your business invests £100,000 in eligible R& D:
</p>
<p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify; line-height:1.8; padding-left:20px;">
●   A £20,000 RDEC (20%) is given to you.
<br>
●   The benefit is approximately £14,700 to £16,200 after taxes.
</p>
<p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify;">
<strong>ERIS Scheme:</strong> The Enhanced R&D projects for R&D-intensive, loss-making SMEs
</p>
<p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify;">
For SMEs that incur losses and invest significantly in research and development, the <strong>Enhanced R&D Intensive Scheme (ERIS) UK</strong> programme provides more advantages.
</p>
<p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify; line-height:1.8; padding-left:20px;">
●   If you are an <strong>SME</strong> (less than 500 employees and less than $100 million in turnover), you are eligible for ERIS.
<br>
●   You’re causing losses.
<br>
●   At least 30% of your overall business expenses go toward research and development.
</p>
<p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify;">
If you qualify, you can surrender your loss for a 14.5% cash credit, which is entirely tax-free, after deducting 186% of your R&D expenses from your loss calculation.
</p>
<p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify;">
<strong>For instance:
</strong>
</p>
<p style="font-size:16px; color:#000; text-align:justify; line-height:1.8; padding-left:20px;">
●   If you spend £100,000 on <strong>R&D,</strong> you subtract £186,000 from your earnings.
</p>

●   <strong>Overseas Costs:</strong> Northern Ireland businesses benefit from ERIS’s greater generosity, which permits claims for employees who work abroad.
<br>
●   <strong>Grace Period:</strong> You might still be eligible for ERIS this year if you passed the R&D intensity test the previous year.
