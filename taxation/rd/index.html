How R&D Tax Credits Work

The UK R&D tax credit regime rewards companies that invest in innovation.

SME R&D Relief

Under the merged RDEC scheme from April 2024, SMEs can claim a 20% credit on qualifying R&D expenditure, providing a direct cash benefit or corporation tax reduction.

Qualifying Expenditure

Staffing costs, subcontractor costs (65% for third parties), consumables, software, and certain cloud computing costs all qualify — if the work meets the technical advancement test.

The Technical Test

Work qualifies if it seeks an advance in science or technology by resolving technical uncertainties. A competent professional in the field must not know the outcome in advance.
